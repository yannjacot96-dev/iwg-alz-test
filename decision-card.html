Why this matters

The same A+ T+ biomarker profile yields a Highly probable diagnosis of Alzheimer disease in a patient presenting with an amnestic syndrome — but only an Unlikely diagnosis in a patient with a Richardson syndrome or dementia with Lewy bodies.

This is the core IWG position: biomarkers are necessary but not sufficient. The clinical phenotype determines the diagnostic weight of any biomarker result.
